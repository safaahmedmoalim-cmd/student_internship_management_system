# User Manual

Student Internship and Attachment Management System – Langata Technical and Vocational College

Open the system in a browser (computer, tablet or phone) and sign in with your **username or email** and
password. After signing in you see the dashboard for your role. The bell icon shows unread notifications.
Use **My Profile** to update your details and change your password, and **Forgot password?** on the
sign-in page if you cannot sign in.

---

## 1. Students

### Create an account
1. On the sign-in page choose **Create your account**.
2. Enter your names, registration number, department, academic year, a username, email and password.
3. You are signed in and taken to your dashboard.

### Submit placement details
1. Open **Placement**.
2. Choose your organization from the list, or select *My organization is not listed* and type the
   organization's name, address, county, contact person, telephone and email.
3. Enter the attachment start and completion dates, choose the attachment period and attach your
   acceptance letter (optional). Select **Submit for approval**.
4. The status shows *Pending* until the administrator decides:
   - **Approved** – your supervisors will be assigned and shown on the dashboard.
   - **Returned for correction** – read the comments, choose **Correct and resubmit**, fix the details.
   - **Rejected** – read the reason; you may submit a new application.
5. To cancel a pending or returned application choose **Withdraw** and give a reason.

### Weekly logbook
1. Open **Weekly Logbook** → **New entry** (possible once your supervisors are assigned).
2. Fill in the week number and dates, activities performed, skills acquired, challenges and solutions;
   attach a file if needed. Submit.
3. Your academic and industrial supervisors review it **independently**. Each review shows its own status
   and comments. The entry is *Approved* when both approve.
4. If an entry is **Rejected**, choose **Correct & resubmit**, update it and submit again.

### Final report
Open **Final Report**, enter the title, choose your PDF or Word file and submit. Your academic supervisor
approves it or returns it with corrections; upload a corrected version if returned. All versions are kept.

### Results
**Assessment Results** shows your academic and workplace assessments once the administrator releases them,
including the score for each criterion, comments and recommendations.

## 2. Academic supervisors

- **Dashboard** – assigned students, logbooks waiting for you, reports to review, upcoming visits.
- **Assigned Students** – search your students; open one to see placement, host organization, supervisors,
  logbooks, visits, attendance, reports and assessments.
- **Logbook Review** – *Awaiting my review* lists entries you have not decided. **Approve** (optional comment)
  or **Reject** (explain what to correct).
- **Supervision Visits** – **Schedule visit** (student, date and time, physical or virtual, objectives).
  After the visit choose **Record** to enter observations and recommendations. Visits can be rescheduled or
  cancelled; the student is notified.
- **Final Reports** – open the file, **Start review**, then **Approve** or **Return** with the corrections.
- **Student Evaluation** – choose **Assess**, score each rubric criterion from 1 to 5, add comments and
  recommendations. **Save draft** to continue later; **Submit assessment** when final (it is then locked).
- **Reports** – progress, logbook and assessment reports for your students, exportable to PDF and Excel.

## 3. Industrial supervisors

- **Dashboard** – interns, logbooks waiting for you, attendance recorded today, evaluations pending.
- **Assigned Interns** – intern profiles and progress.
- **Logbook Approval** – approve satisfactory entries; reject unsatisfactory ones with an explanation.
- **Attendance** – pick a date, mark each intern Present / Late / Absent / Excused, add remarks and
  **Save attendance**. Saving the same day again updates it.
- **Workplace Evaluation** – rate the ten workplace areas (technical competence, communication, teamwork,
  time management, problem-solving, professional conduct, adaptability, initiative, responsibility,
  attendance and punctuality) and optionally upload the signed evaluation form. Save a draft or submit.
- **Reports** – reports for your interns.

## 4. Administrator

| Task | Where |
|---|---|
| Create, edit, deactivate accounts; reset passwords | **User Management** (tabs per role). Leave the password empty to generate a temporary one, shown once. |
| Departments | **Departments** |
| Host organizations; verify organizations added by students | **Organizations** (filter *Pending Verification* → **Verify**) |
| Attachment periods and deadlines | **Attachment Periods** |
| Review placement applications | **Placements** → **Approve / Return / Reject** (reason required for return and reject) |
| Assign supervisors | **Placements** → **Assign supervisors**, or **Supervisor Assignment**. Only industrial supervisors of the host organization are listed. |
| Start / complete / terminate an attachment | **Placements** → **Details** → attachment status buttons |
| Monitor progress | **Students & Progress**, **Logbook Monitoring**, **Supervision Visits** |
| Release assessment results | **Assessments** → *Completed* → **Release** (or **Release all completed**). **Re-open** sends it back to the supervisor. |
| Announcements | **Announcements** → **Add announcement**, choose the audience and optional expiry date. Recipients are notified. |
| Reports | **Reports & Analytics** → choose a report and department → **PDF** or **Excel** |
| Audit logs | **Audit Logs** – filter by module, text and date |
| Settings | **System Settings** – institution name, student self-registration, required logbook weeks |

## Status meanings

| Item | Statuses |
|---|---|
| Placement | Pending · Approved · Rejected · Returned for Correction · Withdrawn |
| Logbook | Submitted · Under Review (one supervisor approved) · Approved (both) · Rejected (either) |
| Final report | Submitted · Under Review · Returned for Correction · Approved |
| Visit | Scheduled · Rescheduled · Completed · Cancelled |
| Attachment | Not Started · Active · Completed · Terminated |
| Assessment | Pending · In Progress (draft) · Completed (submitted) · Released (visible to the student) |
